# Data Deletion Instructions for mongols.app

At **mongols.app**, we are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparency regarding how your data is handled. As part of our Facebook Login integration, you have the right to request the deletion of your data associated with our application.

To delete your user activities and data for mongols.app, please follow these steps:

### Method 1: Via Facebook Settings
1. Go to your Facebook Profile's **Settings & Privacy** menu.
2. Click on **Settings**.
3. In the left-hand sidebar, navigate to **Apps and Websites**.
4. Find **mongols.app** in the list of active apps.
5. Click the **Remove** button next to the app name.
6. A confirmation dialog will appear. You may also select the option to "Delete posts, videos or events mongols.app posted on your timeline."
7. Click **Remove** again to confirm.

### Method 2: Direct Deletion Request
If you would like us to manually remove all data associated with your account (such as profile information, email, and preferences) from our internal databases, please contact our support team:

* **Email:** support@mongols.app
* **Subject:** Data Deletion Request
* **Required Info:** Please provide the email address used for the Facebook login.

Upon receiving your request, our team will verify your identity and purge your data from our systems within **48 hours**. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.
